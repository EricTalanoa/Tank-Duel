01 · Overview

What a session actually looks like

Every diagram on these pages is computed, not drawn. The trajectories come from the same integrator as the prototype — GRAVITY 0.215, MUZZLE 0.160, WIND_K 0.00038, 8 substeps — replayed live in your browser against a seeded terrain. If a shot looks wrong here, it will be wrong in the build.

Balance bug this exercise found

Scripting the match exposed a dead weapon. Heavy Mortar was specced at mass 2.00, which caps its range at ~595 px. Spawns sit ~700 px apart, so the mortar could never reach the enemy from the starting position — a limited-ammo weapon that is literally unusable until someone moves, and nobody moves. Corrected to mass 1.55 (range ~794 px), which makes it a full-power-only weapon at spawn range. That's real tension instead of a dud.

And one emergent behaviour worth keeping

Scripting turn 8 revealed that a Sandbag wall blocks the player who built it just as thoroughly as the enemy. Cyan had to lob at 72° and near-full power to shoot over its own mound, and a low shot rolled back into its own position. That's not a bug to fix — it's the cost that makes a zero-damage defensive shell a real decision. It is documented on page 05 and asserted on page 09.

The loop, in one breath

Two tanks on a destructible hillside, out of each other's reach except by arc. You set elevation and power, pick a shell, and fire. Wind rerolls every turn so no solution repeats. You miss, you read your own dotted trail, you correct, you hit. The terrain you're shooting at is also the terrain you're standing on, which is the whole game.

Match length
8–16 shots per round. The reference match below runs 11.
Turn length
5–20s of thinking, 0.7–1.9s of flight, ~0.5s of settle.
Failure mode
Health 0, or knocked off the bottom of the map.
Skill expressed
Bracketing, wind correction, reading slope, spending limited ammo.

Deliberate omission

There is no tutorial and no tooltip layer. The elevation and power controls are the entire input surface, and the first miss teaches more than any overlay would. If playtesters can't fire a shot within 15 seconds of the aim screen appearing, fix the HUD, don't add a tutorial.

03 · The core loop

Anatomy of a turn

Five phases. Only one accepts input. Everything that can go wrong in this game goes wrong in the transitions between them.

Phases

PhaseDurationInputExits when
AIMUnbounded (or timer)All controls livePlayer fires
FLIGHT0.7–1.9s typicalNone. Camera only.No projectiles remain
RESOLVE1 frame + 9 frame hitstop on direct hitNoneImmediately
SETTLE0.4–1.5sNoneNothing moving, 22 quiet frames, particles thinned out

The rule that prevents most bugs

Input is accepted in AIM and nowhere else, and fire() returns immediately unless phase === 'aim'. Every "I fired twice" and "the turn didn't switch" bug in this genre traces back to a control that stayed live during flight.

What each control does

ControlRangeStepEffect
Elevation0–90° (displayed)1°, or 5° with ShiftInternally 0–180°; Player 2's display is mirrored so both see 45° as "up and toward the enemy".
Power10–1001, or 5 with Shiftv = power × 0.160 px/frame. Range on flat ground is v² / 0.215.
Weapon6 slotsKeys 1–6Changes mass, drag, blast radius, damage, and terrain effect. Mass is the one that surprises people.
Fire——Consumes ammo, spawns the shell 24 px along the muzzle, starts a new ghost trail.

Spawns sit 620–840 px apart, so real engagements live at power 70–82. Full wind shifts impact by ±115 px, which costs roughly 7 power or 2° to correct.

The settle phase, in detail

This is the phase people forget to build, and it's where the game's best moments come from. In order, every frame:

  1. Any tank whose centre is inside solid terrain climbs upward until clear. A tank must never be entombed.
  2. Any tank not touching ground accelerates downward at 0.42 px/frame², capped at 8.
  3. On landing, if the drop exceeded 40 px, apply (drop − 40) × 0.5 damage.
  4. Any tank past the bottom of the map is destroyed outright.
  5. When nothing has moved for 22 frames and fewer than 22 particles remain, hand off.
Why the order matters

Dig-out before gravity. Reverse them and a tank buried by Sandbags will try to fall through solid ground forever, and the settle phase never exits — a soft lock that only reproduces when someone uses a defensive weapon aggressively.

Annotated playthrough

One complete round, terrain seed 1597, eleven turns, played out shot by shot. Every arc below was simulated in your browser when this page loaded. Brass wins at 58 health.

What the match demonstrates

TurnConcept

3Bracketing. Split the difference between a short and a long, and you're on target.
4–5Cover and counter-cover. A zero-damage weapon buys exactly one turn.
6Area denial. Cluster punishes open ground.

Balance rule

Every limited weapon must be situationally better than the HE Shell, never flatly better. If a weapon is just "HE but more", it isn't a decision — it's a resource drain.

Drag-to-aim conflict

Dragging on the field sets elevation and power at once from the vector to the cursor. That's the fastest way to get roughly on target and the worst way to make a 2-power correction. Both must stay live simultaneously: drag to get close, arrow keys to fine-tune. Never let a drag reset a keyboard adjustment, and never let the sliders fight the drag — one shared source of truth, three ways to write to it.

Accessibility requirements

RequirementImplementation
Playable keyboard-onlyAlready true. Every action has a key. Focus ring must be visible on all controls.
Colour is never the only signalBrass and Cyan differ in luminance as well as hue, and the active player is marked by a border, a brightness change, and the deck accent — not colour alone.
Reduced motionprefers-reduced-motion disables screen shake and hitstop, and cuts particle counts by 75%. Trajectories still animate — that's information, not decoration.
Wind offA setup option, not a difficulty setting. Some players cannot track a second variable, and the game is still good without it.
Touch targets44 px minimum on the ± steppers and weapon chips. The current prototype's 26 px steppers fail this and need fixing before mobile.
Telemetry is textAngle, power, wind, and health are real DOM text with tabular figures, not canvas-drawn. Screen readers get them free, and they stay crisp at any zoom.
No timing pressure by defaultTurn timer defaults off.

Responsive behaviour

  • Above 900 px: full layout, field at native 1000×560.
  • 600–900 px: field scales down, weapon chips wrap to two rows of three, wind gauge moves under the sliders.
  • Below 600 px: field keeps its aspect ratio and the control deck becomes the majority of the screen. Elevation and power stack. Drag-to-aim becomes the primary input because the sliders get short.
  • Resize mid-flight: the simulation is resolution-independent — it runs at 1000×560 regardless. Only the CSS size of the canvas changes. Never re-derive sim units from client rect.

Two transitions that don't exist, on purpose

There is no FLIGHT → AIM (you cannot cancel a shot once fired) and no SETTLE → AIM that skips HANDOFF (which is where fall damage and the win check live). Both are tempting shortcuts when you're chasing a responsiveness complaint, and both quietly break the win condition.

09 · Verification

QA checklist

Every item is an assertion derived from a scenario on the previous pages. Most can run headless against sim/ with no browser — which is the point of keeping simulation and rendering apart.

Ballistics

  • HE at 45°, power 70, no wind, flat ground lands within 5 px of 632.
  • Wind +100 shifts a power-70 impact by +115 px ±10.
  • Max range occurs at 45°; 30° and 60° produce shorter, near-equal ranges.
  • No shell passes through terrain thinner than 4 px at power 100.
  • Heavy Mortar at power 100 reaches at least 750 px — it must be able to cross a spawn gap.
  • Two identical seeds plus identical inputs produce byte-identical final terrain.
  • No call to Math.random exists anywhere under sim/.

Terrain

  • Carving at a map edge does not read or write outside the mask.
  • Sandbags never set a solid pixel inside a tank's hull box.
  • Terrain collision is never owner-aware: a shell fired by the player who built a wall collides with it identically.
  • A tank whose centre is inside terrain climbs out within one settle frame.
  • Repainting after a crater touches only the dirty column range.
  • Destroying all terrain under a tank drops it to the floor, not through it.

Turn flow

  • Calling fire during FLIGHT, SETTLE, or ROUND_OVER is a no-op.
  • Holding Space for 3 seconds fires exactly one shell.
  • Settle always terminates — assert an exit within 600 frames from any state.
  • Turn counter increments once per full round, not once per shot.
  • Wind rerolls exactly once per handoff.

Damage & win conditions

  • Blast damage falls off to exactly 25% at the edge of the radius, never to 0.
  • Self-damage applies at full value — no owner exemption anywhere in the damage path.
  • A fall of exactly 40 px does 0 damage; 41 px does 0.5.
  • A tank passing the bottom of the map is destroyed regardless of remaining health.
  • A double KO resolves to a draw, and the draw path is reachable in a test.
  • Health never renders negative.

Interface

  • Every control is reachable and operable by keyboard alone, with a visible focus ring.
  • Player 2's displayed elevation mirrors correctly: internal 135° shows as 45°.
  • Dragging on the field then pressing ← adjusts from the dragged value, not from a stale one.
  • Resizing the window mid-flight does not alter the trajectory.
  • Alt-tabbing for 30 seconds does not fast-forward the simulation on return.
  • prefers-reduced-motion removes shake and hitstop but keeps trajectory animation.
  • Touch targets on steppers and chips are at least 44 px.
Start here
